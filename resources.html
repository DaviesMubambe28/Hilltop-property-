01 / Buying

Know what you own.

A property listing starts the conversation. Independent due diligence should guide your decision.

  • Ask for the title documents and confirm the seller’s authority with a qualified Zambian legal adviser.
  • Check boundaries, permitted use, access rights and any registered interests before committing.
  • Inspect the building, drainage, water, electricity and any backup systems.
  • Confirm the full costs, conditions, deposit arrangements and completion timeline in writing.
  • For land, verify survey information, access and servicing rather than relying on a photograph.

02 / Renting

Start with a better brief.

  • Set a total monthly budget including utilities, service charges and transport.
  • List your essential location, space, accessibility and furnishing needs.
  • Confirm availability, deposit, lease term, renewal conditions and maintenance responsibilities.
  • Record the condition and inventory at handover; retain a signed agreement and payment records.

03 / Investing

Look beyond the price.

  • Define your horizon, liquidity needs and tolerance for vacant periods.
  • Assess location, ownership, permitted use, infrastructure and management requirements.
  • Compare income assumptions with operating costs, maintenance, tax and financing.
  • Test lower income and higher expenses using the rental yield calculator.
  • Seek independent legal, tax and financial advice. Estimates are not promised returns.
