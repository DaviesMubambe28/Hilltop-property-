# Hilltop Perspective design constitution

## Purpose
Help Zambian buyers, tenants, sellers and investors discover relevant property, understand its facts, and request assistance with confidence.

## Non-negotiable principles
1. Preserve the existing Hilltop mark and navy `#062e4b` / gold `#f7c928` identity until genuine replacement artwork is provided.
2. Editorial serif headings; restrained sans-serif labels and controls; one token system.
3. White and warm neutral surfaces, deliberate spacing, fine rules; small corner radii; no decorative gradients or heavy card shadows.
4. Search and property discovery take precedence on mobile. Cards browse vertically; all essential actions have 44px targets.
5. No invented statistics, agents, testimonials, stock-as-property photography, unverified counts or implied return guarantees.
6. Genuine missing data gets an honest neutral fallback. Technical deployment details belong in operational documentation.
7. Native semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, labelled dialogs, announced status and error recovery.
8. Preserve authentication, authorisation, schemas, migrations and all operational workflows.
9. No push, deployment, production data access, paid resources or remote merge.
10. A screenshot is evidence of a rendered implementation, never independent customer validation.

## Acceptance
All seven requested viewport sizes; no material page overflow; preserved behavioural coverage; no serious/critical axe findings. Internal 95/100 is an aspiration, never a fabricated score or superiority claim.
