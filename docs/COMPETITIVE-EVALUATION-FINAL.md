# Competitive evaluation — final evidence boundary

Reviewed 26 November 2026. The cloud browser failed before opening a page, so equivalent rendered desktop/mobile competitor captures could not be produced legitimately in this environment. Search retrieval supplied current page text and listing metadata. The findings below describe discoverable content and product structure; they do not establish rendered visual quality, accessibility, mobile behaviour or performance.

## Evidence-supported findings

- **Pam Golding Zambia** exposes a large, current catalogue with sale/rent categories, favourites, map mode, pagination, email alerts, area profiles and specific property facts. That live inventory and local-market proof exceed Hilltop’s synthetic fixtures. Hilltop’s reconstructed mobile comparison and recovery states are strengths, but not proof of overall superiority.
- **Homes Platinum** exposes property search and Zambia-specific property/category pages. Retrieved text was too sparse to judge hierarchy, photography, conversion or accessibility.
- **Broadleaf** exposes a very large marketplace catalogue, ratings, categories, location facts and explicit anti-viewing-fee guidance. Retrieved listings also show inconsistent long-form titles and repeated image metadata. Hilltop is editorially calmer, but rendered comparison was unavailable.
- **Sotheby’s International Realty, Knight Frank, Christie’s and Savills** expose substantial global inventories, precise facts, market-specific discovery and high-value pricing. Knight Frank also connects listings with buyer guides and local editorial context. Their genuine media, inventory depth and institutional credibility remain a much higher evidence bar than Hilltop’s preview.
- **The Modern House** explicitly centres architectural curation, researched histories, architect context, edited listing copy and collection-based discovery. Hilltop borrows an editorial architectural sensibility but does not yet have comparable provenance or content depth.

No competitive-superiority verdict is supported.

## Manual rendered-comparison checklist

Collect each permitted site at 390×844 and 1440×900 with date, URL and cookie state recorded. For home, catalogue and one detail page:

1. Capture the first viewport and full page without hiding normal banners.
2. Record time to identify search, execute a location/type filter and open a property.
3. Count visible listing facts, calls to action and steps to enquiry/viewing.
4. Inspect image quality, aspect consistency, captions, agent identity, price/location prominence and legal/disclosure language.
5. Test keyboard navigation, focus visibility, headings, form labels, menu/dialog behaviour and 200% zoom.
6. Record console failures, broken media, horizontal overflow and a repeatable throttled performance trace.
7. Separate objective observations from reviewer preference. Do not reuse competitor imagery in Hilltop materials.

Use the same inventory class where possible: a residential property near K8 million or the closest locally meaningful equivalent. Document inaccessible pages, geolocation variants and consent walls instead of bypassing them.
