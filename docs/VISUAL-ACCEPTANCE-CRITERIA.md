Inspect each major page for: clear hierarchy; readable uncut headings; consistent brand/tokens; intentional image crop and honest provenance; visible quick search on mobile; vertical property browsing; no page overflow; keyboard-visible controls; readable status and useful recovery; no fabricated business claims.

Details: bounded gallery, desktop enquiry column, mobile enquiry controls with safe-area padding. Comparison: usable two-property values at narrow widths. Portals: legible unavailable states plus later authenticated verification when credentials exist.

Actual screenshots must be captured after rendering; inspect representative widths plus runtime geometry at all sizes. Record material deviations and blockers. Do not declare authenticated staff/admin visual acceptance from signed-out screenshots.

Score weights: hierarchy 12, typography 10, brand 10, photography/authenticity 10, discovery 15, mobile 15, conversion 10, accessibility 8, performance 5, credibility 5. Evidence per score; unavailable evidence remains unscored, not presumed perfect. 95 is an aspiration.
